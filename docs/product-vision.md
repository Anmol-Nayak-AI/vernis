# Product vision

Vernis gives professional art organizations one defensible artwork identity and a set of evidence-first workflows. It serves artists, collectors, galleries, museums, conservators, logistics teams, insurers, appraisers, and researchers without pretending that one role or algorithm can settle authenticity, rights, conservation, value, or liability.

The product principles are:

1. Preserve unknown, asserted, disputed, reviewed, and verified states explicitly.
2. Reuse the same artwork, asset, evidence, and organization identity across all engines.
3. Make computational observations reproducible and versioned.
4. Require humans for professional or irreversible conclusions.
5. Cite source material and expose limitations near every result.
6. Keep tenant authorization in the API and database, not only the interface.

The six engines are ArtSentinel (due diligence), ArtShield (authorized visual rights monitoring), ConservaVision (condition comparison), MuseGraph (collection intelligence), ArtValuate (licensed comparable research), and ArtTransit (shipment/custody evidence). Their results converge only through the evidence library, reports, and Artwork Intelligence Passport.

Success means a user can trace a statement back to an authorized record, understand whether a human reviewed it, and reproduce the deterministic computation. It does not mean automated certification.
